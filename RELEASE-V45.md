# V45 — Valley Run

V45 makes the Desert theater physically read as a valley mission.

- Start farther south and higher, outside the defended valley.
- Cross a broad southern ridgeline before entering the target area.
- Mission Control explicitly tells the pilot to drop into the valley and stay below the ridgelines.
- The southern ridge is real terrain: collision, SAM radar line-of-sight, and enemy terrain avoidance all use the same surface.
- The Launch Complex remains deep inside the valley.
- After the firestorm strike, the north side is closed by a higher ridge with one lower natural high-pass notch.
- The compass points ENTRY before ingress and PASS during egress.
- Theater transition no longer occurs on an escape timer. EW-01 must physically cross the northern rim.
- V44 sustained fire, secondary explosions, aggressive interceptors, SAM smoke trails, terrain masking, flight model, camera and turbo audio are preserved.

Build: `node scripts/build-v45.mjs`.
