# V52 — Valley Wakes

A narrow opening-mission pass based on live-play feedback.

- The first seconds remain quiet.
- Search-radar pulses are now tied to terrain line of sight during the approach.
- Moving behind terrain removes the pulse, making low flight feel useful without adding a stealth meter.
- A faint non-interactive crossing contact makes the valley feel occupied before the first engagement.
- Launch-site vapor is easier to read from farther away and briefly intensifies when the valley notices EW-01.
- First detection gets a stronger transition cue: "THEY HAVE A TRACK."
- Existing V51 mission guidance, V50 escalation, later strike logic, firestorm, egress, flight model, camera, turbo audio, and controls remain unchanged.

Build with scripts/build-v52.mjs.
