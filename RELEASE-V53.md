# V53 — Basin Reveal

A structural first-minute redesign based on Grok's live-play diagnosis.

- The southern ridge now hides a much deeper defended basin.
- The final approach is visually tightened by flanking shoulders.
- Crossing the crest reveals the launch complex, radar, SAM layout, roads, vapor, and a moving patrol in one wide basin composition.
- The launch objective marker remains ENTRY until the crest so the HUD does not spoil the reveal.
- The HUD briefly steps back during the reveal itself.
- The launch complex and rocket are physically larger in the basin so they read as the center of the scene.
- Two low terrain folds immediately north of the ridge create somewhere for the player to dive and mask.
- The existing detection, radar LOS, SAM logic, fighter pressure, firestorm, egress, flight model, camera, turbo audio, and controls remain intact.

Build with scripts/build-v53.mjs.
