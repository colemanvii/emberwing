# V54 — Knife Edge

V54 makes the basin reveal physically stronger rather than adding more atmospheric polish.

- The launch complex is pulled closer to the ridge so it reads as a real defended place from the reveal.
- The radar site moves with the compressed corridor.
- The southern ridge is sharpened into a saddle/knife-edge crossing.
- Broad basin shoulders frame the defended site.
- The launch complex, rocket, SAM sites, and radar are scaled for better long-range readability.
- The reveal briefly clears some haze so the full basin composition can be read for roughly two seconds.
- HUD locator elements step fully out of the way during the money shot.
- The distant patrol crosses the launch complex itself during the reveal.
- Existing flight model, camera, turbo audio, controls, detection, strike, firestorm, and egress logic remain unchanged.
