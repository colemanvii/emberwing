# V47 — Escalation

- Desert interceptors can now launch air-to-air missiles after stealth breaks.
- Missile firing solutions get easier for defenders as EW-01 lingers or stays exposed.
- SAM reacquisition pressure increases over time without bypassing terrain line-of-sight.
- Later interceptors become more capable as the valley defense network fully alerts.
- Clean, fast, low flying buys the player time; clumsy, exposed, lingering flight makes survival progressively harder.
- V46 stealth opening, V45 valley ingress/egress, V44 firestorm, and player flight/turbo/weapon feel remain intact.

Build: `node scripts/build-v47.mjs`.
