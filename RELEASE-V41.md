# V41 — Strike Mission

V41 turns the Desert theater into a coherent hostile strike mission while preserving V40 flight feel, camera, player weapons, turbo behavior/audio, terrain, Alpine and Tempest.

Mission chain:
1. Low ingress into a defended valley.
2. Defending fighters intercept EW-01 instead of flying away from it.
3. Long-range contact acquisition is clearer through earlier detection, bearing calls and a restrained sun-glint.
4. Three physical SAM batteries defend the launch complex. Terrain masking and low altitude break their line of sight. Radar destruction degrades the SAM network.
5. The radar strike is mandatory and remains physical geography rather than disappearing when the player flies away.
6. After the defending CAP is cleared, the launch vehicle becomes the final ground target.
7. The player must designate and destroy the launch vehicle.
8. The launch site burns while EW-01 performs a short playable northbound egress under surviving SAM threat before Alpine transition.

The bad V40 flag decal remains removed.

Build: `node scripts/build-v41.mjs`.
