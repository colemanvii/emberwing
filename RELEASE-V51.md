# V51 — Mission First

Small guidance pass for the Desert mission.

- Distant aircraft no longer automatically take over the main objective marker.
- Immediate nearby threats still take priority.
- The HUD keeps the player oriented toward the mission route and target unless danger is close.
- Air-to-air targeting controls are unchanged.
- V50 pacing, defense escalation, firestorm, egress, flight model, camera, turbo audio, and weapons remain unchanged.

Build with scripts/build-v51.mjs.
