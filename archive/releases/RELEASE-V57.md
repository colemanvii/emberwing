# V57 — The Price of Visibility

V57 keeps V56's literal fork and makes the terms of the choice readable before commitment.

- The center massif is taller and less negotiable.
- The right route preserves a clear visual channel toward the launch complex.
- The existing radar and one SAM site are seated where they can be seen down the open route.
- The left route is deeper and separated from the basin by a taller terrain spine.
- The launch complex stays visually occluded on the left until a later emergence.
- Right-route detection now depends on an actual clear search-radar line through the terrain.
- Left-route detection remains delayed unless the player climbs out of the wash.
- The right route keeps a readable delay between first detection and full interceptor/SAM pressure.
- Coaching and objective markers step back at the fork so the world itself communicates the choice.
- No new combat system, HUD system, flight physics, controls, weapons, or later-theater changes.

Build with scripts/build-v57.mjs.
