# V46 — Stealth Break

V46 changes the opening rhythm of the Desert valley strike.

- No bandit is waiting in the sky at mission start.
- The first fighter is hidden and suppressed during the quiet ingress.
- SAM batteries are physically present but do not radiate or engage before EW-01 is detected.
- Crossing the ridge and flying low buys a short stealth window deeper into the valley.
- Staying high after the crest exposes EW-01 earlier.
- Detection wakes the network, triggers radar cues, then commits the first interceptor from the defended north.
- V43/V44/V45 aggressive intercept behavior, terrain masking, valley geometry, firestorm strike, and physical egress remain intact.

Build: `node scripts/build-v46.mjs`.
