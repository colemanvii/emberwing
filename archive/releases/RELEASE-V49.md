# V49 — Living Valley

V49 refocuses Theater 1 around one defended objective: the launch vehicle.

## Core changes

- The opening brief is no longer a fighter/radar checklist. The job is penetrate, stay low, destroy the launch vehicle, and escape.
- Radar is opportunistic rather than mandatory. Destroying it degrades the SAM network; skipping it leaves the defense sharper.
- The launch order is triggered by the defender response to penetration: deep intrusion, a radar strike, or prolonged exposure.
- The launch vehicle can be designated and struck while an enemy fighter is still alive.
- Defenders no longer need to be "cleared" before the strike. Their job is to keep EW-01 off the target.
- If the player gives the valley time, a hard defender commits from the Launch Complex and can employ missiles while the target is active.
- Fast, low, decisive flying reaches the target before the network fully organizes. Slow/high/clumsy flying produces stronger fighter and SAM pressure.
- A soft pre-detection search sweep gives the quiet ingress tension without breaking stealth.
- Launch-site vapor intensifies as the defense response matures; emergency launch has a low-frequency rumble and V48 ignition/liftoff cues.
- A surviving defender continues into egress; if none survives, the existing last-ditch egress interceptor can still appear.
- V45 valley geometry, V44 firestorm, V47 hostile missile escalation, terrain masking, flight model, camera, turbo audio and weapons remain intact.
