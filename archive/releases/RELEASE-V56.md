# V56 — The Fork

V56 turns V55's implied route choice into explicit geography.

- A massive central rock body physically splits the end of the throat.
- Left route: lower, longer, and terrain-masked. The launch complex disappears until the player emerges much closer.
- Right route: higher and faster with early sightlines on the basin, but radar exposure comes sooner.
- Random procedural props are pushed out of both authored corridors.
- The launch complex and radar are re-seated around the fork geometry.
- Route choice is inferred from physical commitment, not a menu.
- First exposure on the right route gets extra time between detection and full pressure so the player can read cause and effect.
- The left route preserves masking longer unless the player climbs out of it.
- The HUD stays subordinate to the geography during the split and reveal.
- Core flight, camera, turbo audio, weapons, strike logic, firestorm, Alpine, and Tempest remain unchanged.

Build with scripts/build-v56.mjs.
