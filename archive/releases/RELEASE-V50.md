# V50 — Closing Door

V50 is a directorial pacing pass for the Desert mission.

- Detection escalates from fleeting contact to confirmed tracking to full alert.
- Low terrain flying delays the response; exposed flight speeds it up.
- Surface defenses join progressively instead of all reacting at once.
- The opening aircraft crosses the player's route and creates pressure without becoming a mandatory duel.
- The final firestorm briefly disrupts fresh tracking without making the player safe.
- Surviving aircraft stay relevant during the northern escape.
- Clearing the pass creates a short stretch of open-sky quiet before Alpine.
- Theater 1 HUD reports mission state instead of bandit kill count.
- V49's optional radar, reactive launch sequence, simultaneous air/ground pressure, valley geometry, firestorm, flight model, camera, turbo audio, and weapons remain intact.

Build with scripts/build-v50.mjs.
