# V55 — Long Throat

The first minute of Theater 1 is now an authored level rather than open procedural transit.

- Start inside a broad canyon wash that bends twice before the defended basin.
- The throat narrows from roughly 520 units to roughly 330 units, enough to feel fast without becoming a twitch tunnel.
- Procedural props are pushed out of the authored flight corridor.
- The old south-ridge geometry is cut into a real saddle exit.
- The launch complex is moved much closer to the throat exit and physically scaled for readability.
- The basin presents a real route choice:
  - straight/direct = fastest and exposed
  - west wash = longer and terrain-masked
- A central terrain spine physically blocks radar line of sight into the west wash.
- The first defender comes from the launch basin after detection instead of appearing as an abstract contact.
- Before the reveal, HUD guidance points through the throat, not through rock at the target.
- V53's brief HUD step-back at reveal remains.
- Core flight model, camera, turbo audio, controls, weapons, later strike logic, firestorm, egress, Alpine, and Tempest remain unchanged.

Build with scripts/build-v55.mjs.
