# V48 — Strike Window

V48 makes Theater 1 feel like one authored strike mission instead of a repeated three-bandit loop.

- Quiet valley ingress and stealth break remain intact.
- First interceptor is the only opening air-to-air fight.
- First kill immediately transitions the mission into the radar strike.
- Radar destruction opens a short strike window.
- One final defender scrambles from the Launch Complex as a surprise.
- Killing that defender immediately exposes the launch vehicle as the final target.
- There is no third generic pre-strike bandit.
- Launch sequence now escalates visibly through ignition, hot vehicle, and committed liftoff cues.
- The rocket rises faster if the player delays the final shot.
- V47 enemy missiles and time/exposure escalation remain active.
- V45 valley ingress/egress and V44 firestorm remain intact.

Build: `node scripts/build-v48.mjs`.
